KNOW YOUR HAIR

Understand Your Hair Journey

Hair concerns can look different at different stages. Understanding the stage can help you choose a suitable routine.

1

Healthy & Balanced

Hair looks relatively full, manageable and naturally hydrated. The main focus is maintaining the scalp and hair routine.

Routine: Gentle shampoo + conditioner + occasional oiling.
2

Early Concerns

You may notice increased shedding, dryness, frizz or changes in scalp oiliness.

Routine: Focus on scalp cleansing, conditioning and consistent care.
3

Visible Thinning

Hair may appear less dense in particular areas and the scalp may become more visible.

Routine: Gentle care and professional evaluation when needed.
4

Advanced Hair Loss

Significant thinning or bald areas may be visible. Cosmetic products alone may not address the underlying cause.

Important: Consider consulting a qualified dermatologist.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Start by identifying your scalp and hair type. A basic routine can begin with a suitable shampoo and conditioner before adding oils, masks or serums.
Most hair-care products are selected based on hair and scalp needs rather than gender. Choose according to your hair type and product instructions.
Washing frequency depends on scalp oiliness, activity level, hair type and personal preference. Avoid choosing a schedule solely because of a fixed number.
No cosmetic product can guarantee hair regrowth. Persistent, sudden or significant hair loss should be discussed with a qualified healthcare professional.
